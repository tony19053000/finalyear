## Controller RSS creep - investigation (2026-10-28)

Local fabric: real controller + 4 agents, shipped timing, external PG16 with `pg_stat_statements`, this
laptop (16 CPUs). Runs `ring10k`, `ring1k`, `refit60`, `nodump` (no tracemalloc, no py-spy) and `mem1`
(tracemalloc on the controller, 8 frames).

1. **The Python heap plateaus.** `mem1` (tracemalloc): traced memory 101.6 MiB after wave 0 -> 107.8 MiB at
   wave 9 -> 107.8 / 106.3 / 107.7 / 108.2 at waves 10-13 -> 107.3-108.0 MiB over 8 idle minutes. The growth
   before the plateau is the bounded stores filling: `BusEvent` +7 499 (ring cap 10 000), `AttemptLogState`
   +951 with `StreamMerge`/`FailClosedNotice` x3 (LogHub `_ended`, cap 4 096), `LiveSample`/`DiskSample` (live
   rings, 150 per node) and the strings asyncpg decoded for those events (shown as
   `selector_events.data_received`).
2. **The bounded stores are the slow fill a 50-min soak sees, and they cap.** Every dump now prints them:
   e.g. `ring10k` after 591 jobs: ring 7 923 / 10 000, LogHub ended 591 / 4 096, live samples 199 / 600. At idle
   the ring grows ~30 events/min (`node.metrics` frames only when a value changes), so at 4 nodes it takes
   hours of idle to fill; ~13 events per job fill it faster under load. `ring1k` (ring cap 1 000, full from
   wave 0) shows the same RSS shape as `ring10k`, i.e. the ring is a small part of the creep (~0.47 KiB per
   event from the `mem1` diff -> ~5 MiB at the 10 000 cap).
3. **The step is the first calibration refit, at 30 min.** In `ring10k`, `ring1k` and `nodump` the controller
   RSS is flat or falling in idle until t = 1 800 s, then steps +12.6 / +12.5 / +13.1 MiB in one sample,
   exactly when the log shows `controller.planner.calibration_refreshed` (591 matched runs; refresh interval
   1 800 s, first one interval after start). `gc_objects` does not move at the step, so it is not retained
   Python objects: it is the allocator keeping the refit's transient peak (outcomes read twice + matching in
   a worker thread via `asyncio.to_thread`, i.e. a fresh glibc arena).
4. **The step does not repeat.** `refit60` (`calibration_refresh_s = 60`: 33 refits in 30 idle min, 4
   stored + 29 `no_new_runs` that still read and match everything): RSS oscillates 224-234 MiB with no upward
   trend; least-squares slope over the idle window -5.8 MiB/h (from minute 5: -9.7; from minute 15: -19.5).
5. **Dump-free idle (`nodump`, 45 min, sampler only):** idle before the refit 217.5 -> 211.4 MiB
   (slope -20.9 MiB/h), the refit step at 1 801 s (211.5 -> 224.6), after it 224.7 -> 223.7 MiB over 17 min
   (slope -6.7 MiB/h). The whole-window slope (+15.8 MiB/h) is that single step. Agents: +0.4-1.0 MiB/h,
   flat.
6. **G1's numbers fit exactly:** start-up warm-up (192 -> ~215 MiB in the first 4 min: imports, pools, first
   jobs, executor threads 6 -> 20) + the bounded stores + the 30-min refit step = 210 -> 226 MiB over 50 min.
   A 1 h compressed soak has its first refit in the middle of its window, which reads as "no plateau".

**Verdict: warm-up + one bounded high-water step, not a leak. No product change.** For the soak rule
(D410-3): the 72 h run's 6 h windows start long after the first refit; a 1 h local re-run should exclude
the first `calibration_refresh_s` + one sample, or set `calibration_refresh_s` short so the step happens
inside the warm-up. To re-check: `MESHFORGE_PROFILE_CALIBRATION_REFRESH_S=60
MESHFORGE_PROFILE_IDLE_DUMP_EVERY=0 MESHFORGE_PROFILE_WAVES=8 MESHFORGE_PROFILE_IDLE_MIN=30 pytest -m perf
tests/cluster/test_v10_g4_profile.py` -> `results.json` `memory_idle_slope_mib_per_h.controller` <= ~0.

## Found on the way (not G4 scope - for the Lead)
- **F-1 `registry.write_failed` logged as error with a traceback after an abandoned admission.** In `mem1`
  the tracemalloc snapshot blocked the loop ~16 s, all 4 leases expired, an agent's re-admission was
  abandoned (`session.admission_abandoned`, `row_written` but no session row), and the queued
  `close_session` for that session then failed `UnknownSessionError` (`failure_type: not_found`) ->
  `registry.write_failed` at error level with traceback (twice) -> the cluster harness invariant failed the
  test at teardown. The fabric recovered by itself; behaviour is correct, but a close for a session whose
  open never committed is expected after an abandoned admission and should be a quiet no-op (info, no
  traceback). Paths: `controller/session/ends.py` (~l.300 abandoned admission), `controller/session/writer.py:274`,
  `controller/registry.py:730`. Reproduce: any > lease-length (6 s) event-loop stall during reconnects.
- **F-2 refit reads outcomes twice per refresh** (`calibration_store.refresh`: `_prepare` and again under the
  lock) and does the full read + match even when the answer is `no_new_runs`. Cost measured once `hist4k`
  finishes; fix only if it passes the D412 10 % bar.
