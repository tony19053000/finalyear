  default, `on_invalid: drop` drops it *and counts it* (never silent); (e) hour estimate = hint on the mid laptop
  + 15 s per-run overhead (`RUN_OVERHEAD_S`); (f) one workload seed per group (derived from spec seed + group)
  so every strategy/repetition of a group runs on the same synthetic data.
