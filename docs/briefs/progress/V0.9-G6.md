- Double-click cancel / pause during a pending mutation; 409 `campaign_running` and controller-down toasts.
- Truncated Parquet (export failing mid-stream) must not be saved; huge campaign (many runs) in Runs paging.
- Labels with `insufficient` / `pending` only -> Charts empty state, no client-side statistics.
- Quality against the G5c controller (after merge): crit verdicts while pending, fillers total, CV threshold, outlier count.
