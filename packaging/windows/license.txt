MeshForge Agent

MeshForge is a final-year student project. The team has not chosen a license
for its own code yet; until it does, all rights are reserved by its authors.

This installer bundles third-party software under its own licenses:

* a python-build-standalone build of CPython (Python Software Foundation
  License; its notices are in <install folder>\<version>\python\LICENSE.txt),
* Python packages whose license texts ship inside their *.dist-info folders in
  <install folder>\<version>\python\Lib\site-packages\ (among them pywin32,
  PSF-2.0 / BSD-style).

What MeshForge does on this computer: it runs a background service
(MeshForgeAgent) that connects to the MeshForge fabric you join and runs the
jobs you allow, within the limits you set in the MeshForge console. It accepts
no jobs until you turn that on.
