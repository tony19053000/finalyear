## HERO
hive.
Your laptops, working as one.
hive joins the computers you already own into a single shared machine - and decides, every time, the fastest way to run your work.
[Get started]  [See it work]

## THE IDEA (one line under hero)
Most laptops sit idle. hive puts them to work together - on your own Wi-Fi, under each owner's control.

## HOW IT DECIDES  (3 quiet columns)
Run here - small job? It stays on your laptop.
Send it - your laptop is busy? A friend's laptop runs it and sends back the result.
Split it - big job? hive cuts it into pieces, many laptops work at once, and joins the answer.
An AI planner predicts the time of every option and picks the fastest.

## WHAT IT RUNS
AI training and image recognition on GPUs. Transformers on text. Scientific simulations. Data processing. Anything that can be split.

## BUILT FOR
Students. Small labs. Teams with laptops that sit idle.

## YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Each owner decides what their laptop may run, and how much CPU, memory and GPU it shares. Laptops join with a one-time code and talk over encrypted, certificate-checked connections.

## THE PARTS  (small, one line each)
Agent - a small background app on every laptop.
Controller - the one place that plans and tracks the work.
Dashboard - see every laptop, job and split live.
Command line - for people who like a terminal.
