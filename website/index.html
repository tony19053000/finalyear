Many laptops. One hive.

Pool the computers you already own. hive works out where every job runs fastest.

Download Follow the story

Most laptops sit idle most of the day. Spare power, on the desk next to yours.

So pool them. Over the Wi-Fi you already have, each laptop becomes one cell of a single machine.

Then hive decides. For every job it weighs three ways to run it and picks the fastest, in about 6 ms.

  1. 01

    Stay local

    Small job. Your own laptop runs it.

  2. 02

    Hand off

    Yours is busy. Another laptop takes it and sends the result back.

  3. 03

    Split

    Big job. Pieces go out at once and come back as one answer.

Many laptops, locked into one hive. And it is measurably faster.

Measured, not promised. Real runs on four student laptops, Linux and Windows mixed.
