Each mark shown large, as a favicon at 32/16 px (plus a 6x pixel zoom of the real 16 px raster), mono on honey, and as a horizontal lockup. All type is drawn as SVG paths — no font dependency.

L1

Open Cell

The h is half a honeycomb cell, left open at the base; the full stop is the one closed honey cell. Every letter of the wordmark is cut from the same hex edges, so the wordmark is the logo and the mark is just its first letter.
